A conductor is the wrong shape for a patrol. `sdlc-old-fashioned` run by a conductor needs someone at its gates, and a patrol has no one standing anywhere, so it dispatches the lifecycle's workflow engine instead. Point an unattended ranger at the conductor and the gates are guaranteed to be either improvised past or silently dropped.

These three rules are cross-cutting and unconditional, as listed below.

- Fact-check at every critical decision moment. That means gate rule 6 at triage, and inside the ranger at each root-cause call, each design fork, and the moment an unverified fact is about to enter code. Decompose the claim into verifiable sub-claims, prove each with evidence, and treat the unprovable as false.
- No PR is labeled `ai-done` un-grilled. The script can now actually keep this claim, and the watcher checks it rather than assumes it. `ai-done` requires a grill result. A missing one is reported, not rounded up to done.
- Stealth in the field (Oath rule 8) at every tier. Write no code comments beyond the repo's own house rules, and put nothing in commit messages or PR text that names the Watch, nights-watch, or "ranger". Carry this into the brief or `goal` at dispatch, since it doesn't travel on its own.
