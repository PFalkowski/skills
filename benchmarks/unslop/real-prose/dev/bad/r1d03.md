The conductor is the wrong shape here. `sdlc-old-fashioned` run by a conductor needs someone at its gates. Additionally — a patrol has no one standing anywhere, so it dispatches the lifecycle's workflow engine instead. Pointing an unattended ranger at the conductor guarantees the gates are either improvised past or silently dropped.

Three rules are cross-cutting and unconditional.

- Fact-check at every critical decision moment (gate rule 6 at triage). Inside the ranger, do it at each root-cause call, each design fork, and the moment an unverified fact is about to enter code. Decompose into verifiable sub-claims, prove each with evidence, and treat the unprovable as false.
- No PR is labeled `ai-done` without a grill. The script can now actually keep this claim, and the watcher checks it rather than assuming it. `ai-done` requires a grill result, and a missing one is reported, not rounded up to done.
- Stealth in the field (Oath rule 8) at every tier. Write no code comments beyond the repo's own house rules, and put nothing in commit messages or PR text that names the Watch, nights-watch, or "ranger". Carry this into the brief or `goal` at dispatch, since it does not travel on its own.
