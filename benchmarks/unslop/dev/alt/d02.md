## Cache invalidation for the pricing service

Archived products never had their pricing cache entries expired. After a price change, the catalog kept serving the old price for up to 72 hours.

With this change every entry stores the catalog version. Bumping `catalog_version` invalidates all older entries at once, and the check lives in `price_cache.py`. I made the bump part of the same transaction as the price write, so no reader can pair a new price with an old version, including under concurrent writes.

I tested it two ways. The tests in `tests/pricing` exercise the new path, and I replayed ticket PRC-88 against staging without a single stale read.
