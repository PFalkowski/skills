## Search team status, week 42

The ranking change that shipped on October 6 moved first-result click-through from 31 percent to 38 percent. That fits what we expected, since better search usually means people come back.

The ranker and its scoring step now read features from a single store, which removed the nightly rebuild. Typo handling and seasonal queries both benefit. Searchlight Weekly covered the change.

Next is the spelling corrector. It might raise click-through again, but it might also miss product codes that look like typos. The experiment measures speed, relevance and cost, and Mateo Quill expects results by October 20.
