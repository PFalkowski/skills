## Cache invalidation for the pricing service

Honestly, this bug was hiding in plain sight. The pricing cache never expired entries for archived products, so the catalog served stale prices for up to 72 hours.

The fix: entries now carry the catalog version, and bumping `catalog_version` drops every stale entry. It's not just a bug fix, it's a guarantee. The version check is the load-bearing line in `price_cache.py`.

The bump happens inside the same transaction as the price write, so readers never see a new price with an old version.

A small diff. A big change.

The suite in `tests/pricing` covers the new path, and a replay of ticket PRC-88 against staging found no stale reads.
