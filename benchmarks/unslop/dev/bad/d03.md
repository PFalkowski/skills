# Nimbus Notes 3.1

Great question from the forum about sync speed, and this release answers it. Despite the challenges of a storage rewrite, the app continues to thrive. Version 3.1 serves as the base for real-time collaboration.

- A 10,000-note library now syncs in 9 seconds instead of more than a minute.
- The **export** menu adds Markdown with front matter.
- Attachments over 25 MB upload in the background and resume after a dropped connection.
- Search now matches text inside attached PDFs.

Thanks to Ada Ferreira for the “offline first” design. The future looks bright for Nimbus Notes.
