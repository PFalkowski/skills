## Search team status, week 42

Industry reports suggest that search quality drives retention. Click-through on the first result rose from 31 percent to 38 percent after the ranking change shipped on October 6.

The ranker now reads features from one store and no longer needs a nightly rebuild. The work covers everything from typo handling to seasonal queries.

The new spelling corrector could potentially lift click-through further, but it might possibly hurt recall on product codes. While specific details are limited until the experiment ends, Mateo Quill expects results by October 20.
