## Cache invalidation for the pricing service

The pricing cache never expired entries for archived products, so the catalog could show stale prices for up to 72 hours after a change.

Entries now carry the catalog version, and bumping `catalog_version` drops every stale entry. This also gives us something we can test. The version check in `price_cache.py` is the line everything else depends on.

The part I worried about was ordering. The bump happens in the same transaction as the price write, so readers never see a new price with an old version, even under concurrent writes. The diff is small, but it changes how invalidation works.

For testing, the suite in `tests/pricing` covers the new path, and a replay of ticket PRC-88 against staging produced no stale reads.
