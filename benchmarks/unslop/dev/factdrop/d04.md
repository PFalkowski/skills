## Search team status, week 42

Click-through on the first result rose from 31 percent to 38 percent after the ranking change shipped on October 6. Better results seem to bring people back, which is why we started this work.

The ranker now reads its features from one store, so it no longer needs a nightly rebuild. The change helps with typo handling and seasonal queries, among others.

We are testing a new spelling corrector. It may lift click-through further, but it could hurt recall on product codes. The experiment measures speed, relevance and cost, and Mateo Quill expects results by 20 October, when it ends.
